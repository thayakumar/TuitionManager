# TuitionHub glossary

- **Invoice-eligible lesson** — A completed, attended lesson that has not already been included in an invoice. A free trial can be included without a charge.
- **Billable lesson** — An invoice-eligible lesson with a positive charge.
- **Invoice draft** — A prepared invoice that remains private to the tutor until issued.
- **Billing month** — The Malaysia-time calendar month to which a lesson's invoiced revenue belongs.
- **Submitted work** — A student's current assignment or quiz submission together with its retained earlier attempts.
- **Current submission** — The latest attempt for an assignment. Only this attempt can receive a grade.
- **Submission attempt** — One retained version of submitted work, including its content, attachments and any grade, feedback and grading maximum recorded for it.
- **Attachment transfer** — Placing a file into managed storage with an identified owner.
- **Parent link** — A revocable link granting a guardian access to one student's shared information; it is managed separately from the student's account status.
